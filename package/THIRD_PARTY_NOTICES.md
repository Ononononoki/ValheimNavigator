## LivingMap

ValheimNavigator reads your terraforming and ground paint from the world data the game saves for every edited zone. The idea to read that record for the map, and the way paint is sorted into dirt, cultivated, paved and cleared ground, follow LivingMap by tbsj1ga (https://github.com/tbsj1ga/LivingMapValheim). Navigator's reader is its own code, written from the game's own terrain saving and loading code. LivingMap's license is included here as thanks and credit.
